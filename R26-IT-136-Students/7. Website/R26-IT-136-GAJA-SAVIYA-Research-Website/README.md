# GAJA-SAVIYA · Research Portfolio

> **Safe Routing & Early Warning Framework for Human-Elephant Conflict in Rural Sri Lanka**  
> SLIIT Faculty of Computing — Undergraduate Research Prototype

---

## 📌 Overview

**GAJA-SAVIYA** is an open-data, software-only early warning and safe-routing framework designed to mitigate Human-Elephant Conflict (HEC) across vulnerable transit corridors in rural Sri Lanka. By combining acoustic intent monitoring, environmental risk modeling, and community early warning channels, GAJA-SAVIYA helps rural commuters, farmers, and wildlife share the road more safely.

---

## 🌐 Live Website & Structure

This repository contains the interactive research portfolio web application:

- **Home (`index.html`)** — Project overview, acoustic intent monitoring demo, key capabilities, and impact metrics.
- **Problem & Solution (`problem-solution.html`)** — Deep-dive into Sri Lanka's HEC dynamics and our technological framework.
- **Research Gap (`research-gap.html`)** — Comparison matrix and critical gaps addressed in wildlife tracking literature.
- **About Us (`about.html`)** — Research team members and academic supervisors from the SLIIT Faculty of Computing.
- **Contact Us (`contact.html`)** — Collaboration inquiries and research contact channels.
