GAJA-SAVIYA

An Open-Data, Software-Only Early Warning and Safe-Routing Framework for Human-Elephant Conflict in Rural Sri Lanka

Helping people and elephants share the road more safely.
Read the Problem Contact Us
Overview

What is GAJA-SAVIYA?

Human-elephant conflict is a continuing challenge in rural Sri Lanka where natural wildlife corridors, seasonal farmlands, local roadways, and village settlements closely overlap. GAJA-SAVIYA is an academic research prototype that transforms estimated spatial conflict risk into clear map alerts and proactive, safer route alternatives. Built entirely on open geospatial data and transparent simulation methods, the framework aims to provide community road users with calm, actionable visibility while fully respecting wildlife protection principles.

Architecture

The Four Components

An integrated four-stage pipeline designed to translate raw geospatial signals into safer journeys.

1

Spatial Risk Surface

Estimates relative conflict risk across the landscape by combining elephant habitat suitability with human exposure layers such as roads, settlements, and crop lands.

2

Sparse-Location Tracker

Uses an Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) to estimate an elephant's continuous movement trajectory and associated spatial uncertainty between infrequent or delayed location updates.

3

Road-Risk Assessment

Scores surrounding road segments in real-time by intersecting the baseline risk surface with the tracker's dynamic uncertainty region, triggering automated geofence events.

4

Path Guard

Provides an intuitive interactive map interface that computes and compares the ordinary shortest road route with a risk-weighted safer route, highlighting active warnings.

Workflow

How It Works

From open geospatial data to safer road recommendations in four coordinated steps.

1

Estimate Risk

Synthesize open habitat and community exposure data into a continuous spatial risk surface.

2

Track Movement

Estimate animal position and growing uncertainty ellipses between sparse observations using EKF.

3

Score Roads

Classify nearby road edges according to exposure scores and dynamic proximity corridors.

4

Safer Route

Calculate risk-penalized routes so travellers can make calm, informed routing decisions.

Academic Research Prototype Notice

This is a research prototype. Movement playback is synthetic and clearly labelled. It is not an official public warning service and does not replace the Department of Wildlife Conservation (DWC) or local emergency authorities.
